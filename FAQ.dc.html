It is an all-in-one people and work management platform designed for growing businesses. It helps you manage attendance, leave, projects, meetings, payroll, performance, incentives and everyday employee queries from one place.

It is built primarily for small and medium-sized businesses that want to bring their HR and people operations into one simple system.

Whether you have a small team or are growing quickly, you can use the platform to reduce manual HR work and give employees easier access to information.

The core HRMS includes:

  • Attendance & Time Tracking
  • Leave Management
  • People & Work Management
  • Meetings
  • Payroll & Payslips
  • Performance Engine
  • Incentives
  • AI Assistant

  • Offer Management
