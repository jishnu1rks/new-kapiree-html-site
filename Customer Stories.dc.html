From Spreadsheets to One HR Platform

Company
BrightPath Technologies
Industry
Technology Services
Team Size
85 employees
Simplify HREmpower Employees

The Challenge

BrightPath was managing attendance, leave and employee information across spreadsheets, emails and multiple internal processes.

As the team grew, HR spent more time maintaining information and responding to routine employee requests.

The Solution

BrightPath moved its core people-management processes onto the platform.

They introduced:

  • Attendance & Time Tracking
  • Leave Management
  • Payslips
  • Performance Management
  • AI Assistant

HR was able to spend less time answering routine questions and more time working on people-related initiatives.

Industry
Consulting & Business Services
Team Size
150 employees
Grow with Confidence

The Challenge

NovaWorks was using one system for HR and another set of tools for recruitment. Candidate information was spread across emails, spreadsheets and recruitment platforms.

As hiring increased, the recruitment process became harder to manage.

The Solution

NovaWorks started with the core HRMS and later added the Hiring module.

The recruitment team used:

  • Job Management
  • Candidate Management
  • Applicant Tracking
  • Candidate Pipeline
  • Interview Management
  • Offer Management

The Change

The company could manage its existing employees and recruitment processes within the same platform.

Most importantly, they didn't have to introduce the Hiring module until they actually needed it.
Story 05

Giving Employees an HR Assistant

Company
GreenLeaf Foods
Industry
Consumer & Food Business
Team Size
200 employees
Empower Employees

The Challenge

The HR team was receiving a large number of repetitive questions:

  • How many leave days do I have?
  • Where is my payslip?
  • Who is on leave?
  • What is the leave policy?
  • What is my attendance status?

Although each question was simple, answering them repeatedly consumed HR time.

The Solution

GreenLeaf introduced the AI Assistant alongside its HRMS.

Employees could ask everyday HR questions using natural language and access the relevant information through the platform.

The Change

Employees got faster access to routine HR information, while HR could spend more time on higher-value activities.
