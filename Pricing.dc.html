Features

Website integrationEmbed a branded career page directly on your website so candidates can discover and apply to openings without leaving your site.
Multilingual interviewsLet candidates take interviews in their preferred language. Ideal for global hiring across regional or international teams.
Custom interview questionsCreate your own interview questions tailored to the role — beyond the default question templates. Helps assess role-specific skills more accurately.
Video questions per job postMaximum number of video questions you can add to a single job's interview flow. More questions allow deeper pre-screening per role.
API integrationConnect your ATS, HRMS, or internal tools via API to automate candidate data sync and workflows. Available on paid plans — contact us to get started.

AI Features

Candidate video interviewsCandidates record async video responses to your questions. Reviewable anytime — no scheduling required. Uses credits per interview session.
Interview analysis (AI)AI evaluates candidate responses for confidence, clarity, and relevance — giving you a structured scoring summary to compare candidates faster.
Interview transcripts (AI)Automatically generates a full text transcript of each video interview. Useful for reviewing answers, sharing with team members, and record-keeping.
